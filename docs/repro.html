Reproducibility — Help

Assess consistency across experiments, operators, and conditions

Trend Charts

Line/scatter charts showing concentration means across experiments, grouped by a configurable metadata field (Lysate, Product, Template, etc.). Error bars show ± 1 SD. Use the Group and +Sub dropdowns to customize.

Operator Comparison

Identifies matched conditions — cases where different operators ran the same lysate, master mix, product, template, and reaction volume. Shows side-by-side mean comparisons for each matched pair.

Experiment-to-Experiment Comparison

Paired bar charts comparing mean concentrations across experiments. Helps identify batch effects or systematic shifts over time.

Interpretation Tips

  • CV% < 15% — Good reproducibility (green)
  • CV% 15–30% — Moderate variability (amber)
  • CV% > 30% — High variability, investigate (red)
