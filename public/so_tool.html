Salt Optimization Tool — Help

Process Mg/K screening experiments

Getting Started

  • Experiment ID & Lysate Lot — Enter identifiers in the top bar. These tag your data for retrieval in the heatmap and timecourse views.
  • Excel Results — Upload your Synergy H1 kinetic export (.xlsx). The tool auto-detects multiple timepoint tables and extracts all wells.
  • Layout CSV — Optional. Upload a CSV with Well, MgGlu, KGlu columns, or use the plate builder sidebar.

Plate Builder (Sidebar)

  • Recipe — Set total reaction volume, master mix volume, and lysate volume.
  • Stocks — Enter Mg and K stock concentrations in mM.
  • Mg/K Ranges — Define start, end, and step for each ion. Click Fill Rows or Fill Columns to populate the plate map.
  • Export Layout CSV — Download the current plate map as a CSV file.

Processing

Click Process to run the pipeline: parse Excel, merge with layout, aggregate by Mg/K condition, and identify the top 3 conditions. Results are cached in your browser and available in the Heatmap and Timecourse views.

Related Views

  • SO Heatmap — 2D grid of mean fluorescence by Mg/K condition.
  • SO Timecourse — 384-well kinetic curves with zoom.
